## How to Compile on Overleaf

1. Go to [overleaf.com](https://www.overleaf.com) and create a new project
2. Upload `rms_report.tex` (or `rms_presentation.tex`)
3. Set compiler to **pdfLaTeX**
4. Click Compile

## Personalize Before Submitting

Search for the following placeholders and replace with your info:

- `[Your Name]` → Your full name
- `[Your Department]` → e.g., Computer Engineering
- `[Your Institution]` → e.g., XYZ University
- `[Guide Name]` → Your supervisor's name
- `[your.email@institution.ac.in]` → Your email

## Report Structure

1. Title + Authors
2. Abstract
3. Keywords
4. Introduction (4 subsections)
5. Literature Review (paragraph + 15-paper table)
6. Research Gap (6 identified gaps)
7. Proposed Methodology (6 subsections including workflow, model, algorithm, dataset, technologies, privacy)
8. Expected Outcomes
9. Conclusion
10. References (15 papers + foundational FL papers)

## Presentation Structure (16 Slides)

1. Title Slide

5. Problem Statement
6. Objectives
7. Literature Review Table
8. Research Gap
9. Methodology Overview
10. FL Workflow (Step-by-Step)
11. Model & Algorithm
12. Dataset (IRDRD)
13. Advantages & Expected Outcomes
14. Conclusion
